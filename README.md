# Coffee-sales-time-series-analysis

### **Overview**

This project analyzes time-stamped sales data from a coffee vending machine in Ukraine to improve:

* Maintenance scheduling
* Operational efficiency
* Product restocking strategy

---

### **Objectives**

* Identify peak and low sales periods
* Recommend maintenance windows that minimize downtime
* Determine which products require most frequent refilling
* Extract sales trends from detailed timestamp data

---

### **Data Exploration & Cleaning**

Steps performed:

* Reviewed all features to understand available data
* Removed columns with limited or no analytical value
* Formatted and cleaned timestamps for time series use
* Handled missing or invalid entries where necessary

---

### **Time Series Analysis**

* Examined hourly and daily sales patterns
* Detected low-traffic periods suitable for machine maintenance
* Identified high-demand products and restocking frequency
* Used time series visualizations to interpret trends

---

### **Key Insights**

* Certain hours show consistently low sales — ideal for maintenance
* Specific products drive the majority of revenue and require more frequent replacement
* Time-based trends highlight customer habits and machine usage patterns
